About Tidy

Tidy helps you clean up the photos and videos on your iPhone by finding duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos and large videos. Please read these terms before using the app.

Last updated October 11, 2026

1

Deletion happens only by your action

The app deletes a photo or video only when you choose to delete it and confirm in the iOS system prompt. Nothing is ever deleted automatically.

Deleted items stay in the iOS "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days, during which they can be recovered. Items older than 30 days, or removed from "Recently Deleted", cannot be recovered. We recommend backing up important photos beforehand.

2

Detection is an estimate

Duplicate, similar and blur detection are mechanical estimates based on image features and are not guaranteed to be accurate. The photo marked "Best" is also an estimate, chosen from focus, how faces come out, brightness and similar cues. The final decision about what to keep and what to delete is yours.
