Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Tidy helps you clean up the photos and videos on your iPhone by finding duplicates, similar shots and blurry photos. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Photos and analysis results never leave your iPhoneAll analysis happens on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.
No advertisingNo advertising IDs are collected either.
No usage analyticsThe app does not collect how you use it.

1. Access to your photos

The app asks for access to your photo library to find duplicates and similar shots. All photo and video analysis happens on your iPhone. Neither the photos themselves nor the analysis results are sent to the developer or any third-party server.

2. How faces come out

To pick the "Best" of a set of similar photos, the app uses Vision, the image analysis built into iOS, to give each face in a photo a 0–1 score for how well it came out (eyes open, expression, blur). This also happens entirely on your iPhone.

Only how well a face came out is scored. The app does not recognise who anyone is or record facial features, and no face image or score ever leaves your iPhone.

3. Information stored only on your iPhone

The following is stored only on your iPhone. No one — including the developer — can see it.

  • Photo analysis results (a blur score and a short numeric signature of how the photo looks; the original photo cannot be reconstructed from them)
  • Scores used to pick the "Best" of similar photos (how faces came out, overall look, brightness)
  • The record of photos you chose to keep while swiping
  • Running totals of how many items and how much space you have deleted

All of this is removed when you delete the app.

4. Advertising

The app contains no advertising.

5. Contact form

The contact form uses Google Forms (Google LLC). The email address and message you enter are stored on Google’s servers and used only to reply to you.

6. Sharing with third parties

Information is not shared with third parties except as required by law and as described above under "Contact form".

7. iCloud and deletion

A photo that is not present on your iPhone may be loaded from iCloud for display or playback. This traffic goes through Apple’s Photos service.

Photos deleted from the app stay in the iOS "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, deletion applies to every device on the same Apple ID.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use "Contact us" in the app’s Settings.

9. Updates to this policy

Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
